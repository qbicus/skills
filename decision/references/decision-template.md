# Decision Log Template

Append entries in this form unless the user requests another structure.

## YYYY-MM-DD - Short Decision Title

### Decision
- What was chosen

### Why
- Key reasons this option won
- Constraints or assumptions that mattered

### Alternatives Considered
- Option A: why it was plausible
- Option B: why it was plausible

### Rejected
- Option A: why it was rejected here
- Option B: why it was rejected here

### Impact
- Files, interfaces, migrations, rollout, or follow-up work affected by this decision

### Status
- `final` or `provisional`
